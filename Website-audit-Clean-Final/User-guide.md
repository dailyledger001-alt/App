# Daily Ledger — Complete User Guide

## 1. Introduction

Daily Ledger is an all-in-one, local-first personal planning system. It connects finances, tasks, habits, fitness, meals, groceries, goals, time blocks, calendar items, and monthly reporting in one workspace.

The app stores data in your browser on the current device. It does not require an account or upload your personal information to a server.

## 2. First-Time Setup

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
2. Open **Preferences** from the top-right data menu.
3. Enter your name.
4. Select your currency, week-start day, and weight unit.
5. Choose which modules should appear in the navigation.
6. Add your regular finance, task, fitness, meal, and life-planning information.
7. Export a backup after the initial setup.

## 3. Main Navigation

Daily Ledger contains these workspaces:

- **Dashboard**
- **Financial Planner**
- **Task Tracker**
- **Fitness Planner**
- **Meals & Grocery**
- **Life Planner**
- **Monthly Report**

On mobile or a narrow screen, use the menu button to open the sidebar.

## 4. Dashboard

The Dashboard is the daily command center. It combines information from every enabled module, including:

- today’s open tasks;
- recurring tasks due today;
- habits due and completed;
- planned meals;
- today’s workout;
- monthly income, spending, and available balance;
- cash-flow and spending charts;
- budget usage;
- upcoming calendar items.

Where action buttons are available, items can be marked completed or skipped directly from the Dashboard. The underlying module updates automatically.

## 5. Financial Planner

### Overview

Choose a month to view income received, expenses, left-to-spend balance, savings rate, budgets, bills, savings goals, and charts. Use **Add income** or **Add expense** for quick recording.

### Setup

- **Income sources:** add regular income with an amount and expected arrival day.
- **Budgets:** assign a monthly limit to each expense category.
- **Savings goals:** create targets and record deposits.
- **Bills:** save regular bills with amount, due day, and category.

Marking income received creates an income transaction. Marking a bill paid creates an expense. Savings deposits update the goal and create a linked expense.

### Transactions

Record income or expenses with an amount, category, date, and optional note. Income can be connected to an income source. Use filters to review history, and edit or remove incorrect records.

### Calculator

The calculator page includes:

- Loan & EMI;
- Savings growth;
- Savings goal;
- Quick percentage;
- Debt payoff;
- Business profit.

Calculator results are estimates and do not modify saved finance records.

## 6. Task Tracker

### Variable Tasks

Create one-time tasks with a title, due date, priority, status, and optional notes. Manage them on the To do, In progress, and Done board.

### Recurring Rules

Create Daily, Weekly, or Monthly rules with an interval, start date, and optional end date. Rules generate future recurring tasks automatically.

### Recurring Tasks

Complete today’s repeating tasks, preview tomorrow, and review completed or missed history. Tomorrow’s items remain read-only until their due date.

### Habit Tracker

Create Daily, Weekday, or Weekly habits. Record completion for today, review weekly performance, and monitor current and best streaks.

### Daily Journal

Record a required mood score and optional sleep hours, water glasses, and note. The journal summarizes recent averages and the logging streak.

## 7. Fitness Planner

### Train

Review the current routine and log today’s workout. The anatomy view highlights muscles trained during the week based on logged sessions.

### Routines

Create a weekly routine by entering a workout name, muscle group, and training days. Routines can be edited or removed and appear on the Train page when scheduled.

### Progress

Use Progress to:

- set a weekly workout goal;
- review the last eight weeks;
- view estimated personal records from logged weight and repetitions;
- record body weight;
- review the body-weight trend;
- edit or remove incorrect weight entries.

## 8. Meals & Grocery

### Weekly Plan

Plan Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks for each day from Monday to Sunday. The plan repeats weekly until edited. Meals are saved automatically.

### Saved Meals

Create reusable meals with a name, meal type, and optional ingredient list. Edit, remove, or place a saved meal into the weekly plan.

### Shopping List

Ingredients from planned meals are added automatically and grouped by aisle where possible. You can also add manual items, mark items bought, and remove manual entries.

Similar quantities use common unit handling such as kilograms, grams, litres, millilitres, cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, and counts.

### Pantry

Add ingredients already available at home. Pantry items are skipped when the shopping list is generated. Remove an item from the pantry when it should appear on the list again.

## 9. Life Planner

### Goals

Create goals in Personal, Health, Money, Career, Learning, or Relationships. Add an optional target date and milestones. Update progress with preset percentages or a custom value. A goal is completed at 100%.

### Time Blocks

Plan focused periods by entering a title, category, start and end time, and one or more days. The app prevents overlapping time blocks. Existing blocks can be edited or removed.

### Smart Calendar

Use the monthly calendar to view connected items from across Daily Ledger:

- tasks;
- events;
- meals;
- workouts;
- bills;
- goals;
- time blocks;
- habits.

Use filters to show only selected item types. Select a date to inspect its details or quickly add a task, event, or meal.

## 10. Monthly Report

Select a month to generate a combined report from the saved modules. The report focuses on completed activity and actual recorded results; planned, open, or skipped items are not counted as completed.

Review the on-screen metrics, then use **Download PDF** to create a shareable monthly report.

For accurate reports:

- record real income and expenses as transactions;
- complete tasks instead of only creating them;
- log workouts after training;
- check habits when completed;
- keep meal and journal records current.

## 11. Preferences

Preferences allow you to set:

- display name;
- currency;
- first day of the week;
- body-weight unit;
- modules shown in the sidebar.

Changing a display unit changes how data is shown. Changing the currency label does not perform foreign-exchange conversion on existing amounts.

## 12. Backup and Restore

Open the top-right data menu.

### Export backup

Select **Export backup** to download a JSON copy of your Daily Ledger data. Create backups regularly, especially before clearing browser data or moving to another device.

### Import backup

1. Export the current data first if it may still be needed.
2. Select **Import backup**.
3. Choose a valid Daily Ledger backup file.
4. Confirm the import.
5. Review the Dashboard and major modules.

Importing replaces the current saved data in the browser.

## 13. Recommended Daily Routine

### Morning

1. Review the Dashboard.
2. Check priority and recurring tasks.
3. Review the workout, meals, and time blocks planned for today.
4. Check upcoming bills and calendar items.

### During the day

1. Record income and expenses when they occur.
2. Update task status as work progresses.
3. Mark recurring tasks and habits when completed.
4. Log workouts and adjust meals if plans change.

### Evening

1. Complete the Daily Journal.
2. Review unfinished tasks.
3. Check tomorrow’s recurring tasks and calendar.
4. Confirm that financial transactions and completed activity were recorded.

## 14. Recommended Monthly Routine

1. Review income, spending, budgets, and bills.
2. Check savings-goal progress.
3. Review completed tasks, habits, workouts, and journal consistency.
4. Open Monthly Report and select the completed month.
5. Download the PDF report if required.
6. Export a fresh JSON backup.

## 15. Troubleshooting

- **A Dashboard number looks wrong:** open the related module and check dates, statuses, and recorded values.
- **Finance totals are incorrect:** verify transaction type, date, amount, and category.
- **A recurring task is missing:** confirm its rule, interval, start date, and end date.
- **A habit cannot be checked:** only a habit due today can be marked from its current cell.
- **A planned ingredient is missing:** check whether it is already saved in the Pantry.
- **A time block cannot be saved:** check for an overlapping block on the same day.
- **Monthly Report is missing an item:** confirm that the item was completed or recorded during the selected month.
- **Data is missing on another device:** export a backup from the original browser and import it on the new device.
- **Data disappeared after browser cleanup:** restore the most recent backup.

## 16. Privacy and Data Safety

Daily Ledger stores data locally in the browser. Anyone who can access the same browser profile may be able to view the data. Use a secured device, protect exported backup files, and keep more than one recent backup if the information is important.
